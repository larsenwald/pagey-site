Most people learn to close the door -
drill it, bolt it, treat it with superglue.
Decide the world has taken much more
than it was ever meant to.

But you kept your door open wide,
light spilling out like a miracle,
and somehow never lost the pride
of caring even when it's difficult.

I want you to know I see you.

I feel the shift
every time I'm in your midst.
You are, without even trying, a gift.
Not despite your softness. Because of it.

So here's to you, on this one day,
from everyone who knows what you're worth:
may everything find its way to Pagey,
the kindest door on earth.
