Bright

to my dear Kae, my angel friend
even without a halo that shines bright
in your soul, there, shines pure light
your heart carries the warmth of every sun kiss upon the skin
you gave me memorable times of bliss
that I will, for sure, miss
today feels gentler because it reminds us of your existence
may the kindness that you give always be returned in an outpouring way
just so you can acknowledge how loved you are, day by day
and when I talk about love, I will talk about you
my brightest star, the glow that will never die
i believe in the sun even when it doesn't shine
so, I love you, with all your silences
